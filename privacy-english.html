2What the app does

RESAVO helps you catalog items, estimate their resale value, and create listing copy for selling platforms. RESAVO is a paid service: the app can only be used with an active Pro subscription, and an account is required for that — without an account and a subscription, there is no access to the app's features.

3What data we process

3.1 Photos and details about your items

  • Photos you take with the camera or select from your photo library
  • Details about the item (name, brand, model, category, condition, price estimate, score, status such as "listed" or "sold", sale price and date, chosen platform)
  • Listing copy you edit (title, description)

Because an account is required to use the app, this data is always also stored in our cloud (see 3.3).

3.2 Processing by artificial intelligence

To let the app recognize items, estimate their value, and write listing copy, we send your photos and the details about the item to an AI service (see Section 5). This happens only after your explicit consent, which the app asks for before the first scan. Without consent, no scan takes place.

You can withdraw consent at any time under Profile → Privacy → "Analyze photos with AI". Processing that has already taken place is not affected.

The results (recognition, valuation, texts) are automatically generated suggestions. They have no legal effect on you. You decide for yourself whether to use them.

3.3 Account and synchronization

An account is required to use the app (see Section 2). When you register, we process:

  • your email address and, if you sign in via Google or Apple, the name and identifier of that sign-in service
  • the time of registration
  • your items, photos, and settings (for backup and synchronization across your devices)

3.4 Settings and local data

Your preferences (e.g. country, goal, shipping, tone, preferred platforms, appearance) and cached listing copy are stored on your device and synchronized with your account.

3.5 Technical data

With every request to our AI intermediary (see Section 5), your IP address is briefly processed to limit abuse (rate limiting). We do not store it permanently for this purpose.

3.5b Crash and error reports

So that we can fix bugs, the app sends a technical report to Sentry on crashes and errors: error message, program flow (stack trace), app version, operating system and device model, as well as the steps immediately before the error (e.g. "request to the AI service failed", without content). These reports contain no photos, no text from your listings, no email address, and no account identifier. We do not enable screen recording or session replay, and we do not measure usage performance.

3.5c Product analytics (optional, only with your consent)

With your explicit consent (Profile → Privacy → "Usage analytics"), we use PostHog for simple product analytics: which screens are visited (names only, no content), as well as events around the Pro subscription (e.g. "paywall shown", "purchase started/completed/cancelled"). This uses a random, pseudonymous device identifier as well as your account identifier — never your email address. We do not record screen content (no session replay) and do not capture tap or swipe gestures.

You can withdraw consent at any time under Profile → Privacy; analytics then stop immediately and the associated identifier is reset on the device.

3.6 Purchases and subscription management

For the Pro subscription, which is required to use the app, we use RevenueCat as an intermediary between the app and the App Store/Play Store: purchase status, product/subscription identifier, and your account identifier are transmitted there so your subscription is recognized across your devices. Payment data (e.g. card numbers) is handled exclusively by Apple/Google and never reaches us.

3.7 What we don't do

We do not use advertising tracking services and do not share data for advertising purposes. The product analytics described in 3.5c is purely product-related, opt-in, and not linked to advertising networks. The app does not collect location data and does not use contacts.

6Transfers to third countries

The service providers listed above are headquartered in the USA or process data there. The transfer relies on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (where the provider is certified) and additionally on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). For AI evaluation, the transfer is additionally based on your consent (Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR).

7Retention periods

  • Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud data is stored for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, it is deleted.
  • AI processing: we ourselves do not store the photos sent to the AI beyond the request itself.
  • Error reports: stored at Sentry for the retention period set in the project (typically a few months).
  • Rate limiting: IP addresses are processed only for the duration of the time window (about one minute).
  • Purchase data (RevenueCat): stored for as long as your subscription or account exists, and beyond that for purchase record-keeping.
  • Product analytics (PostHog): stored until you withdraw consent, and for no longer than 12 months.

8Deleting your account and your data

You can delete your account directly in the app: Profile → "Delete account". This irrevocably deletes your account, all data stored in the cloud (items, photos, settings), and all data on this device. If you signed in with "Sign in with Apple" or "Sign in with Google", this also deletes the link between your account and that sign-in with us.

Alternatively, you can request deletion by email to mmayr006@gmail.com.

9Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • Data portability (Art. 20)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)
  • Withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3))

To exercise these rights, write to us at mmayr006@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Austria with the Datenschutzbehörde (dsb.gv.at), in Germany with the data protection authority responsible for you.
